## i18n (two perimeters, two axes)

- **Two language AXES, do not mix:** the INTERFACE language (`users.locale`, cabinet/bot chrome) ≠ the AI-CONTENT language (`profiles.target_language`; the canon for generation is sr).
- **Catalog registry (where things live):** public site — `src/api/public/i18n.py` (`resolve_lang`, default **sr**; the `AUTH_MSG` section — sign-up form responses; `TENDER_STATUS`/`TENDER_PROCEDURE` — code labels; `UI` — public shell chrome, including `aria_lang`/`to_top`/legal link labels) · cabinet — `src/api/cabinet/i18n.py` (`resolve_authed_lang`, default **ru**; `csv_h_*` — export header) · **shared bot↔cabinet↔export vocabulary** — `src/i18n_common.py` (`COMMON`, `common_t`) · emails — `src/i18n_email.py` (`EMAIL`, `email_t`, `norm_locale`, default **ru**) · template showcase data — the `template_library.i18n` column (migration 0036). Catalogs are dict dictionaries (we do NOT introduce Babel), key parity is under test.
- ‼️ **Anonymous sign-in screens resolve the language through the SHOWCASE circuit.** `/signup`, `/login`, `/auth/resend` are the customer's first screen, reached directly from the public pages: for an ANONYMOUS visitor the language is determined by `public.i18n.resolve_lang` (`?lang=` → cookie → Accept-Language → **sr**), not by the cabinet fallback `ru`. The string catalogue stays the cabinet one (`cabinet_context_for_lang`), and the marker is set by `deps.use_public_lang_for_anon` on the routes. The same resolver supplies the locale of the NEW account and the language of the email — otherwise the account would be created in a language different from the form the visitor saw. A signed-in user is not affected. Before the fix, for the same request the showcase returned `sr` and the sign-up form `ru`.
- ‼️ **The language switcher is GET links for an anonymous visitor and a POST form for a signed-in one.** POST `/cabinet/locale` writes `users.locale`, and for an anonymous visitor it redirected 303 to `/login`: a click on "SR" on the sign-up form KICKED the visitor off the form. For an anonymous visitor use `?lang=` + `persist_lang` (the same mechanism as on the showcase; do not introduce a second way to remember the choice).
- ‼️ **One worker, two channels — TWO languages.** A notification that goes out both by email and to Telegram (`subscription_reminders`, the win alert) must take the language separately: email — `u.locale` (the cabinet axis, the `i18n_email` catalogue), Telegram — `COALESCE(uc.locale, u.locale)` (the channel axis, the `i18n_common` catalogue). After 0038 these are DIFFERENT values: a customer may keep the cabinet in `ru` and the bot in `sr`. The test must check exactly the divergence, otherwise the worker will be "simplified" to a single language and half of the scenario will break silently.
- ‼️ **When you localise a channel, localise ALL of it.** Twice in a short time half of a channel stayed Russian while the other half was localised: the type/procedure labels in the email, and the win-alert email after the Telegram part had already been translated. Half the work is worse than none — the customer sees a mix of languages in one message.
- ‼️ **State the fallback EXPLICITLY, not through a module-level `DEFAULT`.** The public catalogue's `DEFAULT` = `sr`, while `i18n_email.norm_locale` gives `ru` — the same `None` would give DIFFERENT languages on one path. Messages normalised by the calling code fall back to `ru` explicitly (caught by a test).
- **Shared vocabulary — exports included.** Funnel statuses and plan names come from `COMMON` in the cabinet, the bot AND the CSV export: the export kept its own dictionary ("tracking" instead of "Studying") and silently diverged from the interface.
- **Localise showcase DATA in a separate column, not nine.** `template_library.i18n jsonb` = `{lang: {name, description, activity_kind}}`; Russian stays in the original columns (the admin contract does not change), `i18n IS NULL` → show the original (user templates — their text was written by a person).
- ‼️ **A tag/label that reflects the search must show the language of the SEARCH.** Outside ru, keyword tags are rendered in the Serbian form from the dictionary (a mirror of `expand_term`), otherwise the showcase lies about how matching works. Different source terms collapse into one form — deduplicate INSIDE a card.
- **The interface locale travels in the HMAC-signed session** (`sign_session(..., locale=)`); a language change must reissue the cookie. Old cookies without the field → a one-off fallback to the DB (under test).
- New chrome strings go only through the catalogue, in all three languages. Do not put raw Cyrillic into templates (a lint guard with a shrinking allowlist; `public_base.html` has left it).
- **Acceptance — the RENDER, not the template:** measure the Russian-only Cyrillic letters (U+044B, U+044D, U+0449, U+044A, U+0451, U+0439) in the render of live production under a signed session `locale=sr|en`. ‼️ A metric confirms only what it measures: “0 Russian letters” coexisted with a triple duplicate of a tag — after the rollout LOOK at the screenshot.
- ‼️ **A feature visible in SEVERAL blocks is fixed with a predicate over all render places.** The translation toggle lived only where `_card_title.html` is included; in "My tasks" and the calendar the title was drawn by its own macro — and the customer saw the translation in only one block out of three. The rule: `grep -rn` the partial/macro, list ALL the places that render the entity, fix them together. **A customer's request is a PLACE, not a path to it:** "give me the badge here" is not satisfied by a link to a page where the badge exists.
- The admin panel is out of i18n scope (operator-facing, stays ru). `trial_service` sends notifications to the OPERATOR — also out of scope.
